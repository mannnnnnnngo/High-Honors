## What it does

- **Today** - your classes, what's on your calendar and what's due, on one screen.
- **Classroom** - every class's work with your status, due dates and grades, plus topics, materials and announcements.
- **Calendar** - all your Google calendars in one month view, with events from Mangoclass on your Mac. Make events, invite people, answer invitations.
- **Gmail** - read, search, write, schedule and save drafts.
- **Chat** - your Google Chat conversations.
- **Mangoclass** - connect the free [Mangoclass](https://github.com/mannnnnnnngo/Mangoclass) menu bar app: your class schedule shows here, and your Google Calendar events show in its panel.
- **Goals, bookmarks and a phone page** for the small things.
- English and 한국어.

## Get the app

High Honors installs straight from your browser, so there's nothing to download. Your computer never asks you to press **Open Anyway**, and it's always the newest version.

| Device | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Mac** | Safari: open the site, then **File → Add to Dock**. Chrome or Edge: the install button at the right end of the address bar. |
| **Windows** | Edge: **⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app**. Chrome: the install button at the right end of the address bar. |
| **iPhone and iPad** | Safari: **Share → Add to Home Screen**. |
| **Android** | Chrome: **⋮ → Add to home screen → Install**. |

Once it's installed, type **High Honors** into Spotlight, the Start menu or your phone's search to open it.

## No JavaScript? Still works

With JavaScript turned off, the site sends you to **[highhonors.pages.dev/basic](https://highhonors.pages.dev/basic)**: plain pages made on the server. You can still sign in or make an account, and see today, your classwork, your calendar (and add events), read and send mail, and connect Mangoclass.

## Privacy, in short

Your mail, messages, classwork and events are read from Google when you open a page. They are **not** copied or kept on the server. No ads, nothing sold, and nothing is used to train AI. The full policy is in **[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)**.
